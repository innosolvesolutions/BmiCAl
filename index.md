# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Quick BMI Calculator respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect

Quick BMI Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal information.

The app works entirely on your device and does not require:

* Account creation
* Login
* Internet access
* Location access
* Camera access
* Contacts access

## How Information Is Used

All calculations are performed locally on your device. No information is sent to any server.

## Third-Party Services

This app does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Quick BMI Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.
